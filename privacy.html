Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

Moon Phase does not collect, store or share any personal information.

Data collection

The app and its widget calculate the moon phase entirely on your device using the current date. They do not use your location, contacts, photos or any other personal data, and they make no network requests.

Analytics and advertising

Moon Phase contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs.

Children

Because no data is collected, the app is safe for users of all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
